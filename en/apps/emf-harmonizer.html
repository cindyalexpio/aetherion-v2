Radio-frequency technology has saturated the modern environment. A tradition of alternative research holds that coherent "counter-phase" fields can neutralize the disorder of ambient electromagnetic pollution.

By generating a precisely tuned resonance that matches and opposes the surrounding emissions, the theory proposes, the net field at a location can be harmonized far beyond the effective radius of any screen.

This sandbox simulates the harmonizing variables: band targeting, emission intensity, distance, coil count and run duration.

Field Notes

Developer's Guide to This Simulator
What this was built for: a gadget-dealer's daydream. Wi-Fi is everywhere, and with it came the fringe idea that radio signals "pollute" a background field and can be cancelled out. This is a pretend console for a box of coils you tune to "neutralise" the ambient hum of your own router.
Understanding the parameters
ParameterWhat it means
Source BandThe radio band you're targeting — the slider spans roughly 0.5 to 5.5 GHz, covering Wi-Fi and phone-style bands. The simulated unit must roughly match it to be effective.
Emission IntensityHow strong the incoming signals are assumed to be, in milliwatts per square centimetre.
Device DistanceHow far away the harmoniser sits from the subject, in feet. Closer = more signal to fight.
Harmonizer CoilsHow many counter-windings the box carries. More coils supposedly produce a wider mirror-phase field.
Run DurationHow many hours the machine is switched on per session.
Reading the results
ResultHow to read it
Exposure ReductionThe simulated drop in signal reaching the subject's position, in percent.
NeutralizationThe "mirror-phase overlap" — how well the counter-signal is imagined to cancel the original, in percent.
Harmonic ResidueWhatever imperfectly-cancelled signal is left over after mirroring.
Quiet RadiusThe size of the area the box claims to "quiet", in feet.
Stress IndexA fictional leftover-intensity number outside the quiet zone — a made-up figure, not a health measurement.
Calibration StatusA verdict on whether your coil settings reasonably match the band you're targeting.

Every number on this screen is fiction. No coil box cancels Wi-Fi, and nothing here affects health.
