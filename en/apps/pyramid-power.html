Pyramid Power Array

Telluric Geometry Archive

Ancient monumental geometry meets experimental field theory. Researchers of the alternative tradition held that the pyramid form concentrates ambient "telluric" energy at its core and apex.

Claims included food preservation, razor-blade sharpening, and altered crystal structure in objects placed within the pyramid's resonant zone — effects attributed to focused energy accumulation.

This sandbox simulates the array variables: base dimensions, apex ratio, material density and the number of stacked units.

Field Notes

Developer's Guide to This Simulator
What this was built for: the pyramid-energy legend — that a simple triangular shell concentrates energy at its centre. This sandbox dresses that folk-science in engineering terms (size, steepness, material, stacking) and throws in its two most famous side-effects: blade sharpening and food preservation.
Understanding the parameters
ParameterWhat it means
Base LengthThe width of the pyramid's base in inches — simply the size of the build.
Apex RatioHow steep the pyramid is. 1.0 = gentle slope, 2.0 = tall and sharp. Controls how much "field" crowds toward the point.
Material DensityA 1–10 scale from wood to stone — how massive the construction material is imagined to be.
Stacked UnitsHow many pyramids are stacked on top of one another. More units = stronger concentration.
Subject HeightThe height, in inches, of whatever is placed inside. Taller subjects protrude further into the concentrated field.
Reading the results
ResultHow to read it
Field IntensityThe concentrated field strength inside the pyramid, in relative units.
EnergizationThe "charge" the subject picks up from sitting inside, driven by its height.
Preservation IndexThe legendary freshness-retention score for food stored within.
Sharpness RetentionThe blade-edge-life bonus — how long knives are imagined to stay sharp.
Apex DischargeThe accumulated charge gathered at the pyramid's point.
Array StatusA plain-language verdict on how well-tuned your stack is.

Every number on this screen is fiction. Geometry does not sharpen blades or preserve food.
