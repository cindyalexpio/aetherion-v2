Crystalline minerals have been described as "frozen resonance" — stable lattices whose geometry resonates at a fixed, measurable frequency. A harmonization research tradition mapped these signatures.

By placing the body's own energy matrix in proximity, the theory held, the mineral's steady vibration could coax an out-of-tune system back toward coherence.

This sandbox simulates the resonance variables: crystal species, mass, placement height and charging time.

Field Notes

Developer's Guide to This Simulator
What this was built for: people have believed stones carry subtle energies since prehistory, and heal-crystal folklore still has a long shelf life. This is a cataloguer's shrine — pick a species, set its size, height and charging time, and watch a made-up resonance meter respond.
Understanding the parameters
ParameterWhat it means
Crystal SpeciesWhich folkloric stone you're working with — Clear Quartz, Amethyst, Rose Quartz, Black Tourmaline, Citrine or Selenite. Each carries its assigned character in the model.
Crystal MassHow heavy the stone is, in grams. A bigger stone is (fictionally) a stronger emitter.
Placement HeightHow high off the floor the stone sits, from 0.5 to 10 feet.
Charging TimeHow many hours the stone is "charged" by exposure to the room before use.
Reading the results
ResultHow to read it
Resonance FrequencyThe stone's fictional note in Hz — one of the classic Solfège-style frequencies mapped to each species.
IntensityThe relative field strength the stone is said to produce, driven by mass and charging time.
AlignmentA descriptor for how well the stone's frequency lines up with its surroundings.
CoherenceThe "lattice purity" score on a 1–100 scale.
Overtone DensityThe charging-saturation figure, in percent.
Lab StatusA verdict on your setup.

Every number on this screen is fiction. Crystals do not emit healing frequencies, and nothing here affects health.
