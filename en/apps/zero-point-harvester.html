The Resonant Chamber

Casimir-Coupled Collector
Resonance Coils (Helical Coupling)
Cavity Gap :: Casimir Plates
[ Ambient Vacuum Flux ]

The collector pairs two closely spaced conductive plates inside a helical coil. As the plates approach one another, vacuum fluctuations between them weaken relative to the outside — a pressure differential the coil is tuned to "tap."

Narrower gaps couple more strongly to the vacuum. Resonance frequency must match the cavity geometry. Coil turns set the draw intensity.

Field Notes

Developer's Guide to This Simulator
What this was built for: a what-if toy around the most poetic idea in speculative physics — that empty space itself is buzzing with energy and a clever metallic box could siphon some off. It imagines how the dials of a hypothetical vacuum-energy collector might interact.
Understanding the parameters
ParameterWhat it means
Plate GapThe spacing between two close metal plates, in nanometres (a nanometre spans only a few atom-widths). The story: narrower gap = stronger vacuum coupling.
Resonance FrequencyThe electromagnetic "tune" the cavity is set to, in gigahertz, claiming peak coupling when it matches the chamber geometry.
Coil TurnsHow many loops of wire wrap the pickup coil. More turns = stronger "draw" the coil exerts on the field.
Ambient FluxThe percentage of background vacuum energy the simulation assumes exists where you are — a wide guess, not a measurement.
Units in ArrayHow many identical collectors you chain together. Total output simply scales with the count.
Reading the results
ResultHow to read it
Harvested OutputWatts recovered from the vacuum by a single unit, clamped at a fictional ceiling.
Q FactorA "quality" score for the resonance — coil turns divided by gap size. High Q is the boast every resonator makes.
Parasitic DrawThe power the collector burns just to run itself — its own electricity bill.
Self-Sustain RatioHarvest divided by draw, as a percentage. Above 100% = the fictional box runs itself; below 60% = it's a net drain.
Array OutputTotal harvest across every unit, in kilowatts.
Operating StatusPlain-language verdict from that ratio: Strained, Stable, or Self-Sustaining.

Every number on this screen is fiction. The vacuum-drawing device does not exist.
