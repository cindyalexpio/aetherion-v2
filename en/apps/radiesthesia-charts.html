Declassified

Pendulum dowsing is among the oldest of the lost practical arts — older, the Archive maintains, than the plough or the loom. For three centuries it was dismissed as superstition, and for three centuries the sensitive practitioner answered with the same demonstration: pose a question, and watch what the pendulum swings. This document preserves the Archive's standard calibration chart — the response key, the L-rod protocol, and the sensitivity scale by which the student measures the instrument that never leaves the hand.

Sheet B · Map Protocol

L-Rod Reading Protocol

  1. Hold both L-rods lightly, elbows relaxed, arms held parallel to the ground.
  2. Walk the map slowly, eyes on the terrain beneath the sheet.
  3. When the rods converge and cross, mark the spot: a line passes beneath them.
  4. When they diverge and splay outward, the line has been left behind.
  5. Three convergences on the same bearing confirm the trace. Record and proceed.
