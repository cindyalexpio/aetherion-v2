Russell's Spiral Elements

The Octave-Sequenced Table

Walter Russell (1871–1963) proposed that every element is the same wave-length of energy folded back on itself in octaves — Hydrogen the seed, all heavier elements its harmonic multiples, each a note of the same cosmic string.

Matter, he argued, never rests: it ceaselessly oscillates between an expanding "heating" pole and a contracting "cooling", gravitative pole, breathing around a void — the zero at the centre of the spiral where the two waves cancel into stillness.

The Archive has no laboratory. It has a chamber: a table where every element is placed by its octave, one wave-length of light folded into the next, and read outward from the void.

Each weight tells the spiral where it belongs. The chamber folds it by whole octaves — doubling the wave-length again and again — until every element lands on its own turn of the eight-fold cycle, closer or farther from the void at the centre.

Hydrogen, the seed, stands nearest the void; the heavy elements ride the outer turns, each one a harmonic of the first wave-length.

Field Notes

Developer's Guide to This Simulator
What this was built for: a single, self-contained page that turns Walter Russell's octave spiral into a place you can walk around in. Every catalogue element is a real atom with a real atomic weight; the octave, the polarity and the void are a fully deterministic fiction computed from that weight the moment you press the button.
The octave formula
QuantityHow it is computed
Spiral Octaveoctave = floor(log₂(W ÷ 1.008)) + 1, with W the atomic weight in u and 1.008 that of the Hydrogen seed. The result runs 1 → 8 across the catalogue.
Position Fractionthe fractional part of log₂(W ÷ 1.008) — the unfinished turn of the spiral, from 0 up to a full revolution.
Radius · Anglethe canvas places each dot at radius = maxRadius × (octave ÷ 8) and angle = fraction × 2π, reading outward from the void.
The polarity rule
RuleWhat it does
Even octaveExpanding polarity, Heating charge — energy flaring outward on Russell's "cushion of light".
Odd octaveContracting polarity, Cooling charge — the gravitative pole folding energy back toward the void.
The Voidthe zero at the centre of the spiral, where the two waves cancel. Always reads, and always reads zero.
