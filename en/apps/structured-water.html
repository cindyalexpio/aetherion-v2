Field Notes

Developer's Guide to This Simulator
What this was built for: a mood-board sandbox around alternative-health folklore's favourite claim — that stirring water a certain way rearranges its molecules into prettier, more "useful" configurations. It lets you play with what that supposed transformation and its price tag might look like.
Understanding the parameters
ParameterWhat it means
Vortex VelocityHow fast the water is spun in the chamber, in RPM. The folklore says a strong vortex "orders" the molecules.
Geometry PointsHow many sides the chamber's internal shape has — from a plain circle up to an octagon. More points supposedly means a more crystalline finish.
Exposure TimeHow long the batch is processed, in hours.
Starting pHThe water's acid/alkaline balance before treatment (7.0 = neutral). The folklore claims treatment nudges it toward neutral.
Water VolumeHow many gallons are processed. Scales the final energy figure, not the chemistry.
Reading the results
ResultHow to read it
Structure IndexThe fictional "hexagonal order" score — how much of the water is supposedly arranged into hexagons.
Cluster SizeThe typical grouping size the water molecules are imagined to form after treatment.
Hydration ResponseA colourful descriptor for how "easy" the fictional water is said to go down.
pH ShiftHow far the batch moved toward neutral, in pH points.
Viable EnergyThe fictional "stored potential" the batch carries, scaled by the volume processed.
Batch QualityA letter-grade verdict on your settings.

Every number on this screen is fiction. No water is actually altered by any of this.
