Wands of Horus

Twin Conductance Divination Archive

In the alternative-resonance tradition, a brace of paired metal rods — one tonic, one earth-bound — held in opposite hands was said to close a circuit through the arms and torso of the operator.

Believers held that the galvanic difference between the two metals, conducted through the body's own moisture, generated a measurable micro-amperage and a subtle "current" the user could perceive.

This sandbox simulates the twin-wand circuit: material pairing, rod geometry, grip span, body conductivity and ambient field.

Field Notes

Developer's Guide to This Simulator
What this was built for: a magician's take on divining-rod lore using two metal wands. Different metals in each hand make a real (if tiny) electrochemical voltage, and this model dresses that genuine galvanic behaviour up as a ritual circuit — the "coherence" and "duality" numbers are fiction, but the two-metal voltage is real physics.
Understanding the parameters
ParameterWhat it means
Right Wand (+)The metal rod held in the right hand — copper, brass, silver or gold. These sit at the "noble" end of the galvanic series.
Left Wand (−)The metal rod held in the left hand — zinc, aluminum, iron or copper. These sit on the more reactive side.
Rod LengthPhysical length of the wands, in inches. Longer rods (fictionally) strengthen the field geometry.
Grip SpanThe distance between your hands, in inches. The longer the "circuit", the higher the resistance.
Body ConductivityA 1–10 scale from Dry to Moist. A sweatier grip means better electrical contact.
Ambient FieldThe assumed background energy in the room, as a percentage.
Reading the results
ResultHow to read it
Generated CurrentThe simulated current flowing through the body circuit, in microamperes (µA) — a millionth of an amp, derived from the real voltage difference between the two metals.
Potential DifferenceThe electrical "pressure" between the wands, in millivolts, taken from the galvanic table values of the materials.
Body Circuit ResistanceThe resistance the circuit offers, in ohms, driven by grip span, conductivity and rod length.
Field CoherenceA fictional "geometry" score built from rod length and span.
Duality IndexA fictional score for how "balanced" the pair is, from the voltage and coherence.
Circuit StatusA verdict on whether your chosen pair even produces a measurable current.

Every number on this screen is fiction. The wands produce no health or energy effect — this is for entertainment.
