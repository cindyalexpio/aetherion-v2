Developer's Guide
How it works: The scanner uses a curated botanical registry of over 60 species across 8 plant types, 9 flower colors, 5 climate zones, and 6 leaf shapes. When you scan or describe a plant, the algorithm finds the best matching entry from this database.
Camera mode captures the image and performs identification based on your manual follow-up. The camera helps with framing — the identification itself uses the characteristic matching engine.
Offline capable: All data is stored locally. No API calls or internet connection required.
