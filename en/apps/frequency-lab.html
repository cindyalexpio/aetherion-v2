Frequency Lab

Vibrational Entrainment Archive

All biological systems oscillate. A long tradition of harmonic research held that exposure to precisely chosen frequencies could "entrain" an out-of-balance system back to a healthy baseline rhythm.

The Solfeggio scale — a set of mathematically related tones — was claimed to correspond to specific states of coherence, each tone acting as a tuning fork for a different aspect of function.

This sandbox simulates the entrainment variables: base tone, session length, emission intensity and listener state.

Field Notes

Developer's Guide to This Simulator
What this was built for: a bedtime app built on the "Solfège frequencies" music myth — the claim that certain tones (396 Hz, 528 Hz and friends) carry named healing meanings. Pick a tone, set the session, and watch a fictional relaxation meter climb.
Understanding the parameters
ParameterWhat it means
Base ToneThe main frequency you're emitting, from the seven classic "healing" tones. Each carries a folkloric label such as Transformation or Connection.
Session LengthHow long the session runs, in minutes.
Emission IntensityThe volume/strength of the tone, in percent.
Starting CalmWhere the listener begins on a 1–10 scale — how relaxed they are imagined to be before the session.
Reading the results
ResultHow to read it
EntrainmentThe percent of the listener's brainwaves the tone supposedly aligns ("entrains") to its frequency.
Vibrational IndexThe listener's post-session 1–10 state, nudged upward from Starting Calm.
CalibrationHow closely the tone is matched to the session's aim.
Alpha DepthScore in the "relaxation band" — the calm, awake-but-drowsy brainwave range.
Theta DepthScore in the "absorption band" — the deep-meditation and dreaming range.
Session StatusA verdict on the state achieved.

Every number on this screen is fiction. Frequencies cannot heal, and nothing here is medical advice.
